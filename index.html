Services designed for your success

Personalised Training Programs

We start with an initial physical analysis to understand your current fitness, potential injuries and limitations, crafting an exercise program that's perfect for you.

One size fits nobody… at least not very well. I'll design a program built specifically around your body, goals and lifestyle. We'll also use various methods to track your progress effectively.

Sports Massage Therapy

Whether you're dealing with aches, looking to prevent injury, improve mobility, boost athletic performance or just to get a better nights rest, sports massage can help.

I always do my best to offer training sessions at a time that suits you, whether it's more convenient to meet you at your local gym, or you'd prefer to come to my dedicated training space near Manor House, we'll make it happen.

As the old saying goes, you can’t out-train a bad diet. That's why all personal training programs include bespoke nutritional advice, designed to complement your training and support your overall well-being.
